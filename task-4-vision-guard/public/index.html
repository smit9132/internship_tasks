BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Camera access & consent

Vision Guard requests your browser’s camera permission only after you start. While monitoring, video frames are analyzed on this device using a face-detection model. No images or video are uploaded, recorded, or saved.

Face counts can be wrong and are not proof of misconduct or a way to identify anyone. Monitoring events are signals for human interpretation. You can stop at any time; stopping ends the camera stream.
YOUR DATA

Privacy & data

Camera: requested by your browser after consent. Frames stay in this browser and are not uploaded or recorded.

Detection: MediaPipe Face Detector runs locally. The model and runtime are loaded from public CDNs; those servers may receive ordinary network metadata, never camera frames.

Metadata: alert history lives in this page session. If MongoDB is configured, session times, counts and alert metadata may be stored there. No identity data or images are stored. Without authentication, this is a local demo, not a private multi-user service.

Stop monitoring to stop the camera. Use “Clear history” to remove current alert history or the confirmation prompt to also clear optional server session history.
